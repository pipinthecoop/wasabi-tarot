# Original Midnight Parlor artwork

These new theme assets were generated for this project. They do not use private deck art. The existing dawn/midnight card illustrations are unchanged.

- card-back.png / card-back.webp: optimized derivative of celestial-card-back.png, an original 1024x1536 celestial gold/ink-blue card back.
- header.png / header.webp: optimized derivative of celestial-stone-panel.png, an original 1536x1024 carved dark-stone and antique-gold velvet frame.
- stone-button.png / stone-button.webp: optimized derivative of celestial-stone-button.png, an original 2172x724 transparent-alpha carved stone/gold button with crescent/star endcaps and a blank center for accessible HTML labels.
- spread-ritual.png / spread-ritual.webp: an original built-in image-generation illustration, 1536x1024 before optimization. Prompt: three face-down elegant celestial gold-foil tarot cards on deep ink-blue velvet, antique-gold fine borders, moon/compass/constellation motifs, warm candlelight at edges, atmospheric navy shadows, balanced composition; no letters, numbers, logos, watermarks or references to private artwork.

PNG derivatives support native Slack image blocks; WebP derivatives reduce browser transfer size.
